TicTacTask

Never let a project starve while you focus on another task. TicTacTask keeps you honest to your goals and stops time from quietly slipping away:

  • Know exactly where your time goes.

  • Make steady progress on multiple goals.

  • Stop postponing the things that matter to you.

How does it work?

Want to 30 minutes per day?

  1. Name your task ().
  2. Set a goal (30minutes per day).
  3. Click Add.

Then:

  • Press when you begin.
  • Press when you take a break.
  • Press when you don't need the task anymore.

Start tracking now (free, no signup)
Your data stays in your browser only. No accounts needed.
Switching browsers or clearing data will delete all your tasks and your progress.
All tasks will stop if the browser tab is closed for more than 2 minutes.
